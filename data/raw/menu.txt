tutorial1,1,Use the WASD keys to maneuver. Press the SPACE key to attack. Press TAB to close out of this dialog.
tutorial2,1,Most enemies are hostile on sight. If you wait for them to move toward you then attack at the right timing\,\ you should easily be able to beat most enemies 1 on 1. You will gain experience if you kill an enemy in melee\,\ depending on level difference.
tutorial3,1,Pick up a torch from the basket on your left. Remember to use the WASD keys to interact.
tutorial4,1,In safe areas you will find useful services like buying and selling goods\,\ swords for hire\,\ and life insurance. Most friendlies can be interacted with WASD keys. The character to your right is a supplier.
tutorial5,1,Some weapons provide special skills. If you wield the stick\,\ you will get access to a new skill called lunge. Press TAB to access your menu then press 4 to access your skills menu to read about the skill.
tutorial6,1,Some enemies have special skills and should be avoided until you know what you are doing. Use arrow keys to view tile and press Enter to get target description.
tutorial7,1,If you are wielding something already\,\ you will notice that you cannot carry any more weight. That is because you only have 2 strength right now. Press Q on an empty tile to drop your current item then press Q on the stool while empty handed.
tutorial8,1,If you examine the window you will notice it has an armor value of 1. That means you need at least 2 damage to break that window. The stool on your left gives you 1 damage. Pick it up and smash the window.
tutorial9,1,You can check your current health from the menu. You should always travel with full health. You can heal by resting at a bed or a sleeping bag. Press Q to interact with the sleeping bag.
tutorial10,1,You cannot travel far away places empty handed. You need food to satiate your hunger or you will take starvation damage. Hunger can be checked from TAB menu. Take some food from the crate and press SPACE to interact with it.
tutorial11,1,The world out there is dangerous. You will encounter tough enemies using strange skills\,\ be ambushed by multiple enemies\,\ fight in difficult landscapes\,\ and get lost in the dark. Good luck!
