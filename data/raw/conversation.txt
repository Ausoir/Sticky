Controls,[Use WASD and Left/Right mouse buttons. Press TAB for Menu.]
Tip 1,[You can use right click to pick up chairs and ladders and other doodads but they will not be as effective as a proper weapon.]
Tip 2,[You can interact with your environment by clicking on them when you are near or by pressing down when you are on them.]
Tip 3,[In menu, you can use right mouse button to view button description. You can also use 1 ~ 6 button to select menu buttons. For example, TAB 1 will open the Character menu quickly.]
Tip 4,[Most environmental objects can be destroyed to obtain materials. Note that objects made of wood are hard to destroy and metals are even harder.]
Tip 5,[Whenever you travel to another map you will become thirsty. You will need to find or buy water or alcohol to travel again.]
Tip 6,[Getting insured will allow you to revive after death. You can find an insurance agent at a town, but not all towns have them. In that case, travel to another town if you can.]

Offense,[You can view weapon attack and damage by right clicking your weapon at the Equipment menu. Attack represents your chance to penetrate armor. Penetration chance is equal to Attack:Defense ratio. Damage is how much Hit Points a single attack can decrease.]
Defense,[You can view armor defense and durability by right clicking them at the Equipment menu. Usually your Armor Durability decides your total Hit Points as well as Defense.]
Martial Arts,[You can learn martial arts from Manuals that you can buy from the librarians at a university. Martial arts include Block, Sprint, Lunge, Leap, Tackle, and Flurry. You will need Strength equal to the number of martial arts you know.]
Enemy Types,[Enemy types divide into two types: Humanoid and beasts. Strength of humanoid enemies depend on their gear, and they usually have high intelligence. Beast enemies are stupid and have 1 attack and damage, but usually see farther in the dark.]
About Rent,[Your room will not be accessible 7 days after purchase. You will need to pay again to access your room. You are using the same room no matter where you access the room from, so do not worry about losing anything in your room.]
