tutorial8,1,If you examine the window you will notice it has an armor value of 1. That means the window decreases incoming damage by 1. That means you need more than 1 damage to break that window. The stool you picked up provides +1 damage\,\ so if you wield it you would deal 2 damage in total. Smash the window with the stool.
tutorial9,1,You can check your current health from the menu. You should always travel with full health. You can heal by resting at a bed or a sleeping bag. Press Q to interact with the sleeping bag or use WASD keys to interact with the bed.
tutorial10,1,You cannot perform time consuming actions like resting or leveling up when hungry. Hunger can be checked from TAB menu. Take some food from the crate. Wield the food then press SPACE to interact with it. If your inventory is full\,\ drop some items on the ground.
tutorial11,1,You can press F11 to enter fullscreen mode.
tutorial12,1,You can also use the mouse to play this game. Click on your character to access the menu and click adjacent to your character to attack.
tutorial13,1,Even the easiest enemies can be difficult in some environments. You might find it difficult fighting an enemy against the wall or fighting multiple enemies at once. Note that attacks from behind ignore all defense.
tutorial14,1,The world out there is dangerous. You will encounter tough enemies using strange skills\,\ be ambushed by multiple enemies\,\ fight in difficult landscapes\,\ and get lost in the dark. Good luck!

skills,3,Here are listings of few martial techniques that should be approached with caution.,[Reach,!sk.reach],[Lunge,!sk.lunge],[Parry,!sk.parry],[Constitution,!sk.constitution],[Side Step,!sk.side step],[Hide,!sk.hide]
sk.reach,1,Foes with reach can attack you from 2 tiles away. Try to lure them into a closed area\,\ because they cannot attack directly in front of them. After a successful knockdown\,\ move directly into them\,\ because they will retaliate faster than you can attack them again. Reach is usually provided by spear type weapons.
sk.lunge,1,Foes using lunge will move forward when attacking\,\ so it is important to approach them from the diagonals. Do not attempt to attack in succession. When knocked down\,\ step away as soon as you get up. Lunge is usually provided by sword type weapons like Sticks.
sk.parry,1,Foes using parry will ignore the first direct attack hit. Attacks from the sides bypass this effect\,\ so having allies would help a lot against parrying enemies. Likewise\,\ the block skill provide defense from non backstab attacks\,\ so have an ally hit the blocker from behind.
sk.constitution,1,Foes with constitution\,\ such as lepers and the undead\,\ will not get knocked down. If you want to avoid taking damage\,\ try to one shot them.
sk.side step,1,Foes using side step will knock you down on contact and swap places. This can be lethal when facing more than one foe or if the foe has the assassination skill\,\ which deals 2 extra damage from behind.
sk.hide,1,Foes with the hide skill will become invisible when standing still. For example\,\ the killer ivy in the woods are invisible until you get too close. This effect can be dispelled.
