Urban Destinations

Houses - Residential buildings stand all throughout the urban districts. People do not lock doors, but they will try to kill you if they see you in their houses.
Shopkeepers - There are many different shopkeeps in every urban area. Most notable ones are the Grocery, Restaurant, Tailor, Armorsmith, Weaponsmith, Laboratory, Library, Range, Mercenary, and the Salon shopkeeps.
Innkeeper - Innkeepers are usually located in three story square building. They provide resting services, which provide you with food, motivation, and recovery.
Trainer - Trainers can teach you useful feats, if you have the time, money, and motivation.
Broker - Brokers will buy certain items from you. They are located in the Merchants guild hall.
Insurer - Insurers are located in the Medical guild hall. They will provide services to revive you after death. Note that only Medical guild members can use this service.
Doctor - Doctors are located in the Medical guild hall. They will cure you of any curses and provide synthetic appendages in case of amputations, for money.
Transporter - Transporters are located in the Merchants guild hall and will transport you across maps for a fee.
Officer - Officers are located in the Outposts. Officers will offer you mercenaries for hire. Mercenary guild members only.
