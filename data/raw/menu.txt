skills,3,Here are listings of few martial techniques that should be approached with caution.,[Reach,!sk.reach],[Lunge,!sk.lunge],[Parry,!sk.parry],[Constitution,!sk.constitution],[Side Step,!sk.side step],[Hide,!sk.hide]
sk.reach,1,Foes with reach can attack you from a tile away. Try to attack them from the sides. After a successful knockdown\,\ move directly into them\,\ because they will retaliate faster than you can attack them again. Reach is usually provided by spear type weapons.
sk.lunge,1,Foes using lunge will move forward when attacking. Just like with reach\,\ approach them from the diagonals. Do not attempt to attack in succession. When knocked down\,\ step away as soon as you get up. Lunge is usually provided by sword type weapons.
sk.parry,1,Foes using parry will ignore the first direct attack hit. Attacks from behind bypasses all armor\,\ so attack them from behind.
sk.constitution,1,Foes with constitution\,\ such as the lepers and the undead\,\ will not get knocked down. If you want to avoid taking damage\,\ try to one shot them.
sk.side step,1,Foes using side step will knock you down on contact and swap places. This can be lethal when facing more than one foe or if the foe has the assassination skill\,\ which deals critical damage against prone enemies.
sk.hide,1,Foes with the hide skill will become invisible when standing still. For example\,\ the killer ivy in the woods are invisible until you get too close. This effect can be dispelled.
