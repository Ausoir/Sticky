Offensive Variables,[You can view weapon attack and damage by right clicking them at the Equipment menu. Attack represents your chance to penetrate armor. The chance is equal to Attack:Defense ratio. Damage is how much Hit Points a single attack can decrease.]
Defensive Variables,[You can view armor defense and durability by right clicking them at the Equipment menu. Defense will protect you at a Defense:Attack ratio chance. Armor and Headgear Durability is added on to your total Hit Points.]
Martial Arts,[You can learn martial arts from Manuals that you find from Dojos. Martial arts include Block, Sprint, Lunge, Leap, Tackle, and Flurry. Each art require 1 more Strength than the previous, starting with 2.]
Enemy Types,[Enemy types divide into two types: Humanoid and beasts. Strength of humanoid enemies depend on their gear, and they usually have high intelligence. Beast enemies are stupid and have 1 attack and damage, but usually see farther in the dark.]
Crafts,[You can craft various items by interacting with the Table. If you have spell knowledge you can craft spells at a Desk.]
Medical Items,[The most essential medical items are Bandages and Medicine. Bandages stop bleeding and Medicines stop Sickness. They can both be crafted at a Table.]
Food,[You need food to sleep or travel maps. The easiest way to obtain food is via the grocery merchants.]
Gold,[Gold is the main currency for money here. Find a Dealer or explore dungeons to earn money.]
Weapons,[You can buy weapons from a blacksmith or loot them from humanoid mobs.]
Armor,[You can buy clothing from tailor and armors from the blacksmith. Armor gives you Hit Points and Defense. Hit Points represent how much hits you can endure before dying. Defense represents your ability to shrug off an attack.]
Accessories,[Accessories or headgears are equipment that you can wear in addition to armor. Some accessories can be activated via the <R> key.]
Artifacts,[Artifacts are spellcrafts that you can find in dungeons. They can be disassembled at a Disassembling Table to obtain spell knowledge.]
Spellcasting Basics,[There are dozens of spells that you can cast by crafting a spell scroll, wand, or staff. Spells can be crafted at a Desk, but you need the crafting knowledge.]
Obtaining Knowledge,[If you find an artifact, you will not be able to see what spell it casts unless you have the spell knowledge. Find a Disassembler Table and disassemble the unknown artifact to obtain knowledge.]
Basics of Power,[There are dozens of power in this world. Some powers are activated via the <SPACE> key and some are passively affected.]
Obtaining Power,[You must first become a member of a skilled guild and pass a test to obtain the class that the power belongs to.]
